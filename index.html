About this session

Final placements are the point at which two years of coursework, internships, and preparation meet an actual recruitment process. What that preparation involves, and what the interviews themselves are like, is rarely described in any detail.

  • What the two years at SOIL looked like, and which parts proved most relevant when placements began.
  • How each of them prepared, and when that preparation started.
  • The recruitment process at each company, stage by stage.
  • The questions that came up in the interviews, and how they approached them.

Audience questions will be addressed at the end of the session. This session is ideal for MBA aspirants, students preparing for placements, and anyone considering the programs at SOIL.
