Sudeep George

Sudeep George is a student of the PGPM 2026-27 batch at SOIL Institute of Management, where he serves on the Student Placement Committee. About this session

Final placements bring together classroom learning, internships, and preparation for the real world of recruitment. But what does the journey actually look like?

  • Classroom and internships: how classroom learning and internships contributed to placement preparation.
  • Preparation: strategies and approaches to the recruitment process.
  • Selection rounds: company-specific selection rounds and interview experiences.
  • PGPM and PGDM: differences in placement journeys across the 1-Year PGPM and 2-Year PGDM programs.

The session will conclude with a live Q&A, where participants can interact with the students and get their questions answered. Whether you are considering management education or preparing for placements, it is a first-hand look at the journey from campus to corporate.
