About this session

Artificial Intelligence is changing how we learn, think, make decisions, and prepare for the professional world. This session looks at what that means for the future of management education.

  • The AI-driven classroom: how AI is changing teaching methods, learning experiences, and classroom engagement.
  • Rethinking management education: how management learning must evolve in the age of AI.
  • Skills for the future: the growing importance of critical thinking, adaptability, decision-making, and human judgement in an AI-driven world.
  • SOIL’s approach: preparing future managers for an AI-driven professional landscape.

The session will conclude with an interactive Q&A, giving participants an opportunity to engage directly with Professor Rao and get their questions answered.
