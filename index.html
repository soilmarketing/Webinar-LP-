About this session

Final placements bring together classroom learning, internships and preparation for the real world of recruitment. But what does the journey actually look like? Three SOIL students representing both the PGDM and PGPM programs share their preparation strategies, interview experiences and the role SOIL played in their placement journeys.

  • Classroom to career: how classroom learning and internships contributed to placement preparation.
  • Preparation: strategies and approaches to the recruitment process.
  • Interview experiences: company-specific selection rounds and interview experiences.
  • Two programs, two journeys: differences in placement journeys across the 1-Year PGPM and 2-Year PGDM.
  • Live Q&A: interact with the students and get your questions answered.

Whether you are considering management education or preparing for placements, gain first-hand insights into the journey from campus to corporate.
